EYE MAZE

Steer a point of light through the maze with your eyes.

  1. Look left, right, up or down — the ball rolls that way.
  2. Look at the centre of the screen — the ball stops.
  3. Keep your head still; only your eyes steer.

Your camera is processed locally in this browser by an on-device face model. Video is never recorded, stored or uploaded.

Best with a laptop webcam at eye level, about an arm's length away, face evenly lit. Test mode (keyboard, no camera)
